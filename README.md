## Products

### The 47 Business Prompts — $9

47 complete, copy-paste prompts across five business domains. Every prompt uses
the same skeleton so the output is repeatable instead of random:

```
Role / Task / Input / Format / Tone / Do-NOT
```

The `Do-NOT` block is the part that stops generic output. Contents:

- **Writing & Content** — 12 prompts (articles, SEO descriptions, cold email, video scripts, FAQs, translation, proofreading)
- **Marketing & Sales** — 10 prompts (launch sequences, objection handling, ad variants, avatars, pricing, win-back)
- **Customer Support** — 8 prompts (triage, refunds, de-escalation, knowledge base, churn, onboarding)
- **Operations & Finance** — 9 prompts (SOPs, hiring, invoicing, budgets, vendors, risk registers, contracts)
- **Strategy & Thinking** — 8 prompts (devil's advocate, five whys, pre-mortem, canvas, SWOT, decision matrix, scenarios, pitch feedback)
- **Part 3** — how to build a prompt library, version it, and price AI services

Delivery: Markdown (editable) + styled A4 PDF + commercial licence, ZIP.

### The 12 AI Automation SOPs — $14

Twelve operating procedures for automations small businesses actually buy. Every
SOP has the same 7 parts: purpose, triggers, inputs, steps, decision points,
failure handling, outputs.

Lead capture → CRM · cold outreach with follow-up · reply detection · invoice
reminders · appointment reminders · weekly content batch · ticket triage · status
reports · meeting actions · competitor monitoring · nightly backup · client onboarding.

Delivery: Markdown + styled A4 PDF + commercial licence, ZIP.
